Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

This website, pudasainimohan.com.np (the “Site”), is the personal website of Mohan Pudasaini (the “Owner”). It is operated in a personal capacity and is not an official website of the World Customs Organization (WCO) or of any other organisation. This policy explains what information the Site collects, how it is used and the choices you have.

1. Who is responsible

The person responsible for the processing of personal data on this Site is:

Mohan Pudasaini Brussels, Belgium Email: mail@pudasainimohan.com.np

2. Information the Site collects

a) Information you provide

  • Contact: if you email the Owner or message via WhatsApp or LinkedIn, the information you send (name, email address, message) is used only to reply to you.
  • Workshop registration forms: the registration forms for past training workshops (name, email address, organisation, designation, mobile number and prior knowledge of the subject) are collected through Google Forms and stored in the Owner’s Google account. They are used only to administer the workshop concerned and are not shared with third parties or used for marketing.

b) Information collected automatically

  • Analytics: the Site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit and which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects technical information such as pages viewed, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, and referring site. The Owner does not use analytics data to identify individual visitors. You can prevent this collection with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.
  • Server and network logs: the Site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. These providers may process your IP address and request data for security, caching and abuse prevention, in accordance with their own policies ( GitHub, Cloudflare).

c) Content from other services

Some pages embed content from third parties. When such a page loads, your browser connects to that service, which may set its own cookies or record your visit under its own privacy policy:

  • Facebook / Meta (embedded posts from WCO and Customs administration pages)
  • YouTube / Google (embedded videos)
  • Google Fonts (web fonts) and Google Forms (registration forms)
  • Content delivery networks – Cloudflare cdnjs and jsDelivr – which serve scripts and stylesheets used by the Site (for example the world map and photo viewer)

The Site’s own search function runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing to a server. Share buttons are plain links and do not track you unless you click them.

3. How information is used

Personal data is used only to:

  • respond to messages you send;
  • administer workshops you register for;
  • understand, in aggregate, how the Site is used and improve it;
  • keep the Site secure.

The Owner does not sell personal data, does not use it for advertising, and does not combine it with other sources to profile individuals.

4. Legal basis

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, processing is based on the Owner’s legitimate interest in running and improving a personal professional website (analytics, security), on your consent where you choose to contact the Owner or register for a workshop, and on the performance of that registration.

5. Retention

  • Messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry.
  • Workshop registration data is kept for the duration of the workshop and related follow-up, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Analytics data is retained for the period configured in Google Analytics and is then deleted automatically by Google.

6. Photographs

The Site contains photographs taken at workshops, conferences and other professional events. They are published to document professional activities. If you appear in a photograph and would prefer it to be removed or your face obscured, please email the Owner and it will be done promptly.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to receive a copy of it. To exercise these rights, email mail@pudasainimohan.com.np. If you are in the European Economic Area and believe your data has been handled unlawfully, you may also complain to your national data protection authority (in Belgium, the Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit).

8. Children

The Site is intended for a professional audience and is not directed at children under 16. No data is knowingly collected from children.

9. Links to other sites

The Site links to external websites (for example the WCO, UN ESCAP, IMF and partner organisations). The Owner is not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy on this Site: mail@pudasainimohan.com.np
