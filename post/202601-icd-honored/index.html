Honoured by the Honourable Finance Minister of Nepal

27 January 2026 1 min read Customs

I was honoured to be recognized on the occasion of International Customs Day 2026 by the Honourable Finance Minister of Nepal, in the presence of the Finance Secretary, secretaries from various ministries, senior Customs officials, representatives from the private sector, and members of the media.

This recognition for my contribution to the global Customs community as a WCO-accredited expert is a proud and humbling moment. I am sincerely grateful to the Ministry of Finance and Nepal Customs, and this honour further strengthens my commitment to serve the Customs community.

Finance Secretary Mr. Ghanshyam Upadhyaya felicitating me with a marigold garland.

Director General of Nepal Customs, Mr. Shyam Prasad Bhandari, congratulating me on the recognition.

Receiving the Certificate of Honour from the Honourable Finance Minister of Nepal, Mr. Rameshwor Prasad Khanal

The Future of Data-Driven Vigilance in Customs

International Customs Day is celebrated each year on 26 January and recognizes the critical functions of Customs administrations, such as societal and economic security, trade facilitation, and economic prosperity. Every year, the World Customs Organization (WCO) puts out its International Customs Day theme to underscore the most recent challenges and opportunities facing Customs. The WCO theme for 2025 is "Customs Delivering on its Commitment to Efficiency, Security, and Prosperity". This theme indicates the commitment to the development and modernization of Customs processes around the globe.
