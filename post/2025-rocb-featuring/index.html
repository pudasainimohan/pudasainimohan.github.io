Featured in the WCO ROCB Asia/Pacific Newsletter – Issue 90

24 November 2025 1 min read Customs

I am delighted to share that my article from the WCO BACUDA Scholarship Programme has been featured in the World Customs Organization (WCO) ROCB AP (Regional Office for Capacity Building for the Asia/Pacific) Newsletter – Issue 90. In this article, I have explained how the BACUDA Scholarship was truly instrumental in strengthening both my personal growth and professional contribution to Customs administration. The programme expanded my technical capacity in data analytics and AI, sharpened my problem-solving mindset, and empowered me to design innovative solutions that directly support evidence-based Customs operations. It also broadened my global perspective through collaboration with officers from 12 different countries.

The Future of Data-Driven Vigilance in Customs

International Customs Day is celebrated each year on 26 January and recognizes the critical functions of Customs administrations, such as societal and economic security, trade facilitation, and economic prosperity. Every year, the World Customs Organization (WCO) puts out its International Customs Day theme to underscore the most recent challenges and opportunities facing Customs. The WCO theme for 2025 is "Customs Delivering on its Commitment to Efficiency, Security, and Prosperity". This theme indicates the commitment to the development and modernization of Customs processes around the globe.
