Biography

Mohan Pudasaini is a World Customs Organization (WCO) accredited techincal and operational advisor on Customs Risk Management and Data Analytics, with over a decade of experience in Customs and the public sector. He specializes in international trade and Customs matters, bringing deep knowledge and practical insight into risk-based enforcement and trade facilitation. As a WCO expert, he has conducted multiple international capacity-building missions. He currently serves as a Project Officer at the WCO. Prior to joining the WCO, he worked at Nepal Customs as a Risk and Data Analyst

Mohan also has expertise in SQL, R, Python, and Power BI, enabling him to transform complex data into actionable insights. He actively contributes to international best practices in Customs administration and remains committed to advancing analytics and risk management for more efficient and resilient Customs.
