Delivered WCO Risk Management and Data Analytics Workshop in Uzbekistan

WCO Risk Management and Data Analytics Workshop for the State Customs Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan

I had the privilege of co-facilitating the WCO Risk Management and Data Analytics National Workshop for the State Customs Committee of the Republic of Uzbekistan. The event was held in Tashkent from 16 to 19 June 2025.
